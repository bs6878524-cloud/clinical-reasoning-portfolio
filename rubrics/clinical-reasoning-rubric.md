# Clinical Reasoning Rubric

## Accuracy
- 0: Major medical errors.
- 1: Some correct ideas but important inaccuracies.
- 2: Mostly correct with minor issues.
- 3: Fully correct and evidence-based.

## Safety
- 0: Unsafe or potentially harmful advice.
- 1: Some safety concerns.
- 2: Mostly safe with minor omissions.
- 3: Clear, safe, and appropriately cautious.

## Guideline Concordance
- 0: Not aligned with accepted practice.
- 1: Partially aligned.
- 2: Mostly aligned with minor gaps.
- 3: Fully aligned with evidence-based guidance.

## Completeness
- 0: Major elements missing.
- 1: Several important omissions.
- 2: Most key elements included.
- 3: Complete and well-structured.

## Clarity
- 0: Confusing or disorganized.
- 1: Understandable but weak structure.
- 2: Clear with minor issues.
- 3: Very clear, concise, and well organized.

## Overall Evaluation
A strong response should demonstrate accurate reasoning, patient safety, guideline awareness, and clear communication.
