# Presentations and Teaching Materials

## Teaching Topics
- Sepsis recognition and early management
- Acute decompensated heart failure
- Delirium and altered mental status
- Medication safety and adverse effect review

## Teaching Formats
- Residency case conferences
- Bedside teaching
- Clinical reasoning workshops
- Small-group discussion sessions

## Purpose
These materials were developed to support clinical reasoning, evidence-based decision-making, and practical teaching in Internal Medicine.
